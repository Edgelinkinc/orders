How to Place an Order

Five steps, about two minutes. You can move back and forth between steps before submitting.

  1. Store InfoChoose your store from the dropdown, then enter your first and last name and your NTID. The order date fills in automatically. Picking your store is what routes the confirmation to the right manager and coach, so get this one right.
  2. HandsetsBrands are grouped in collapsible sections. For each model, tick Glass, Male Case or Female Case. Use the Notes box on the right of any row for quantities or colors. If a device isn't listed, use + Add Device at the bottom of that brand.
  3. CablesTick what you need. Ticking an item opens a notes box under it — use it for quantity.
  4. Audio & MiscSame as cables. Buds, power banks, mounts and everything else lives here. Ticking an item opens a notes box under it — use it for quantity.
  5. Review & SubmitCheck the summary, add any overall notes for the inventory team, then hit Submit. You'll see your order number on screen — write it down.

Before You Submit

Four things that save everyone a phone call.

Quantities go in the Notes boxA tick means "we need this." If you need ten, say so in the notes — otherwise HQ is guessing.
Watch for the EOL badgeA device marked EOL is end-of-life. Stock may be limited or gone, so add a backup in your notes.
One order per runPut everything you need in a single order rather than submitting three small ones. It ships together and it's easier to track.
Your NTID mattersIt's how HQ knows who to come back to with questions. Use your real one, not the store's.

What Happens Next

You don't need to chase anyone — updates come to you by email.

1 · Confirmation
Your store, manager and coach get an email straight away with everything you ordered.
2 · Fulfilled
HQ processes the order and marks it fulfilled. You'll get an email when that happens.
3 · Shipped
When it ships you get tracking numbers you can click straight through to the carrier.
